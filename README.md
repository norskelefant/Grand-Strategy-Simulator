# Grand-Strategy-Simulator
A simulator/calculator of the video game Hearts Of Iron 4, simulating various parts of the game like economy and production. 

Things i plan on adding: 
- Being able to simulate the factory counts of countries depending on the strategy used.
- Simulating the arms production of countries depending on the strategy used.
- Being able to compare different strategies to see the statistics over time, such as factory counts and equipment numbers.
- Being able to pick from specific popular strategies or costum strategies within the frames of Hearts Of Iron 4.
- Implementing dynamic parts of Hearts Of Iron 4, such as production efficiency, consumer goods factories, damaged factories due to war, research, upgrading equipment, infrastructure, construction speed and others. 
- Simulating for more countries, taking unique focus trees, decisions and starting factories and equipment into account.
- Maybe a war simulator to compare different types of equipment. 
- Maybe expanding to mods like Old World Blues and Kaiserreich.
